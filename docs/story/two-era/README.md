## What this is

Reworking The Hollow Men around two influences — **FF6** and **Chrono Trigger** — by adding
a **revolution-era NOX** the party can travel to, and restructuring the game from three acts
into **episodes**.

The short version of the premise: the truth the present-day Bureau buries — *magic is human,
the power was always yours* — was **public knowledge during the revolution**, printed on
broadsheets, and the party watches it get buried. The city's charter, the Gold Coat, and
WARDEN all get their origin on screen.

## Ground rules carried from `story-lore`

- NOX is perpetually dark and rainy — in **both** centuries.
- The entity is never explained fully; descriptions break down by design.
- The Bureau's horror is procedural, never cartoonish. Its ancestor institutions inherit that.
- Entity-adjacent enemies were people. In the era, they were people too.
- Invented world facts are **flagged** and require approval before becoming canon.

---

## Scope reality (checked against the repo, 2026-11-15)

The present-day game is ~1/3 of Episode 1, running on **one placeholder tileset**, with
**4 of 10** characters built. The era would roughly **double** the content footprint.

**Therefore:** design fully now, build after Episode 1 is playable end to end. Overhauling
now is cheap precisely because so little is built to discard. Scope the era by **tileset
count**, not feature list.
