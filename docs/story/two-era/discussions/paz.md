# Discussion — Paz

**Question (B2):** She has known about the era for years. Why say nothing? Can she cross back?
**Status:** resolved in discussion · 2026-11-20
**Forced by:** decision 15 — Paz is past-native and self-travelled. Relocating her to the
revolution left a hole her file never had: *she left a century, and nobody said why.*

**Constraint held throughout:** her file has her deciding her politics at twelve and never
softening. Whatever drove her out must be compatible with someone who stayed angry — not someone
who fled and went quiet.

---

## Why she left — the revolution lost, and she was on a list

Her songs were on the barricades. Then the moderates won, the radicals she wrote for were purged
as counter-revolutionary, and **the quieting began** — performed on true believers, as mercy.

She wrote **the first banned song** (already proposed in `approaches.md`). Her channel is the one
thing the new Office of Civic Output has no category for: nobody had yet thought to meter
performance. That made her simultaneously **unregisterable and highly visible.**

So she is not leaving a lost argument. **She is leaving a list she is on.**

### The detail that makes it hurt

Her crossing is the tether rule working exactly as written. She was unmetered, so nothing held
her. Her comrades were practitioners — metered, registered, anchored.

> Everyone who could have escaped the quieting was already too metered to leave.

She is the only one the door worked for. That is not a comfort.

**Rejected:** she was present at the Night of the Lamps (strong guilt, but it makes her silence
about protecting the Bureau's best argument, and crowds the General's night); she fell through by
accident (no motive); she was sent (plot machinery the era doesn't need).

---

## When she left — during the purge, before the signing `[DECIDED]`

She runs mid-collapse. **She never sees how it formally ended.**

### Why this timing and not the other

If she had watched the charter signed, the party's central mystery would be answerable from the
moment she joins, and every later scene would have to invent reasons she won't answer it.

As written, she has the **emotional truth and not the documentary proof**:

| She has | She does not have |
|---|---|
| It was public. We printed it. We said it out loud. | The charter, the acronym, the Office's founding text |
| The radicals were purged | How the quieting was formally authorised |
| The quieting started, on people she knew | The first gold coat, the signing, the Aurum clause |

Her withholding becomes defensible rather than absurd: she does not have evidence, she has a
memory nobody would believe — and the last time she trusted a movement, everyone she loved was
quieted for it. **The era visits remain necessary.** She cannot spoil the reveal because she left
before it happened.

---

## Can she go back — yes, and she never has `[DECIDED]`

Her door is findable. Nothing physically stops her. She has had years of nights to walk back
through it, and hasn't.

This is the decision that converts her silence from a plot device into a character fact:

- Her exile is **a choice she re-makes every day**, not an accident that happened to her. What
  keeps her here is grief, not physics.
- She is not withholding because the information is useless. She is withholding because **saying
  it out loud makes it real that she left them.**
- The iron rule gets its sharpest personal edge: *she can go back; she cannot change it.* She has
  known that for years, which is exactly why she stopped going.
- When the party finally crosses, **Paz is the only one who is not discovering anything.** She is
  the one walking back into the century she abandoned, with people watching.

**Rejected:** her door closed behind her (clean, but makes her a passive refugee); she doesn't
know and won't look (true to a purge survivor, but lets her off a hook the game should keep her
on); she has been going back regularly (undercuts door rarity).

## Knock-ons

- **Recruitment.** Her beat is no longer "she arrives independently at the same conclusion." It is
  **she has been withholding**, with reasons Reid will not accept.
- **Iris.** The music beat doubles as written in `roster.md`: Iris loves anonymous old folk songs
  *and* Paz's current band, and has never connected them. The oldest songs she knows are not
  traditional — and one of them is banned.
- **Āio.** Both are era-native and both watched extraction survive a regime change. Āio was never
  moved by the broadsheet; Paz *wrote* it. They are the same wound argued from opposite ends.
- **Kos.** The recognition beat survives untouched — she noticed him in the deep Meridian as an
  adult.
