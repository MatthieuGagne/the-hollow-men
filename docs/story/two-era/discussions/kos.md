absence kills characters — so what is he *doing* while the party is in the other century?

**Held from his file:** the calibration didn't take and something came back the other way; he

## The spine: he runs the present-side case `[DECIDED]`

The investigation does not pause because half the party stepped out of the century. Kurtz is
accelerating the harvest, people are still disappearing, and **Kos works the present with whoever
else didn't cross.**

### Why this one

- It converts him from *the man who can't go* into **the man who stayed to work.** Agency instead
  of exclusion.
- It gives decision 16 its payoff: a present-side team to cut against the era-side team, so
  episodes can run two fronts instead of marching ten people through one door.
- The present never goes quiet while the party is away — which is what keeps the era from feeling
  like the real game and the present like a hub.

**Considered and parked, not rejected:**

| Option | Why it is being held |
|---|---|
| **He holds the door** — the only one who can feel the wound from this side, acting as anchor/lifeline | Gives him presence in every era visit, but makes his exclusion a *job*, which softens it — and implies a crossing mechanic the doors do not currently have |
| **He gets worse** — what came through is louder each time the party returns; his condition is the manifestation clock | Strongest horror use, and probably true. Risk: makes him a barometer rather than a person. Revisit as a *cost* layered on the spine |
| **He tries to get out** — quietly hunting someone who can reach a deep-bonded sigil | Active and desperate, sets up a reversal. Dangerous: if he ever succeeds, decision 16 collapses. Likely the thing he will not admit he is doing |

Proposed shape when we return to it: **B as the spine, C as the cost, D as the thing he won't
admit.** Not decided.

## The wound this implies (not yet adopted)

His file leaves the wound blank. The travel rules suggest one for free:

> It is not *the entity holds me*. It is **the institution is still inside me**, below any layer I
> can reach, and it is the thing locking the only door out of this century.

He was processed. The paperwork is still executing. And the cruelty is structural: his mechanic
grows him from entity contact, and the revolution is where the entity sits nearest the surface —
**the one place he would grow strongest is the one place he can never go.**
